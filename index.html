Pebbloom

A calm physics puzzle on still water.

Touch a shape, pull back and let go. It glides, bounces off the rim and knocks the others along. Bring every lily pad, blossom, leaf and shell home to its own slot.

  • 60 levels in 6 chapters, each with one new idea.
  • A par for every level. Chase three stars, or don't.
  • Small flick limits with one undo and a free, instant retry.
  • Three hint steps on every try, and an aim line while you pull.
  • A daily tray and an endless Zen mode.
  • No account, works offline, and your progress stays on your iPhone.

Pebbloom is for iPhone. It is coming to the App Store.
