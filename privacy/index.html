The short version

Pebbloom does not collect any data about you. There is no account and no sign-in. Your progress stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

The game saves your level progress and stars, your daily and Zen progress, the tray you are playing (so you can continue where you left off), a few lifetime counts such as trays solved, which tips you have already seen, a random number used to shuffle Zen trays, and your settings (sound, haptics, appearance, Reduce Motion). This is stored only on your iPhone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Apple's own device backups (iCloud or computer backups) may include app data, depending on your Apple settings. That is between you and Apple; we do not receive it.

What Pebbloom does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifier.
  • No analytics, tracking or profiling.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No data sold or shared with anyone.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • No network requests from the game. It works fully offline.

Links to this website

The Settings screen has links to this privacy policy and to the support page. Tapping one opens the page in Safari. The game does not send anything when you do this. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit it, under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not use cookies or analytics on this site.

TestFlight beta versions

Beta versions shared through Apple's TestFlight may keep a local test log on the tester's phone: counts and kinds of play events (for example how many flicks a level took, or which hint step was used). It has no names, no dates and no device identifiers. The app never sends it. A tester can choose to send it to us with Settings > Send test log, which opens the iOS share sheet. The App Store version of Pebbloom does not contain this test log.

Information from Apple

Apple may collect information about downloads, crashes and usage under your own Apple settings (for example "Share with App Developers"); Apple's privacy policy covers that. From Apple we only receive crash reports and summary statistics that do not identify you.

Children

Pebbloom is a puzzle game for a general audience. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under laws such as the EU and UK GDPR you have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Because Pebbloom does not collect personal data, we hold none about you. You can remove everything the game saved by deleting the app. If you email us, we use your email only to reply and delete it when the conversation is done. For any privacy question or request, contact Hasan Bayhan at trillypops.app@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

Future versions may add features such as advertising, optional purchases or downloadable levels. Some of these may involve processing data (for example by an advertising partner). Before any such version is released, we will update this policy to say exactly what is collected, by whom and why, and update the App Store privacy information. We will also tell you about the change in the app or on the App Store listing. The effective date at the top always shows the latest version of this policy.
