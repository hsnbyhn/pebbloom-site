Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Write to trillypops.app@gmail.com.

It helps to add your iPhone model, your iOS version, the Pebbloom version and the level number. We usually reply within a few days.

How to play

Touch a shape, pull back and let go. A longer pull is a harder flick. Slide back to the shape to cancel. Shapes bounce off the rim and knock each other. Bring every shape home to its own slot: when a shape stops close to its slot, the slot glows and draws it in.

Questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone only. Pebbloom has no account and does not sync between devices. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted too (an iPhone backup may bring it back when you restore that backup). If you leave in the middle of a tray, it reopens exactly where you left it.

What do par and stars mean?

Par is the fewest flicks our solver found for that tray. Solve it at par for three stars, a little over for two, any other way for one. Every solve counts and opens the next level, so stars are only there if you enjoy chasing them. After solving, tap "See the par route" to watch it. Levels 1 to 5 and Zen do not show a par.

How do hints work?

Tap the bulb button. You get three hint steps on every try: first which shape to flick, then which way, then a replay of the par shot. Hints are free: they never cost stars or flicks. Retry gives you all three again. If you are no longer on the par route, the third step shows the par route from the start and offers a fresh try.

What is the aim line?

While you pull, a soft ghost of your shape shows where it will first touch another shape, a stone or the rim. With a gentle pull, it shows where the shape will stop instead.

Why can I only use a few flicks?

From level 2, each level has a flick limit, shown at the top. You get one undo per try, which also gives the flick back. Retry is always free and instant, with no lives and no waiting. After three tries that run out of flicks, you can choose "Play without the limit": solving that way gives one star and opens the next level, and you can come back to beat the limit later.

When is there a new daily tray?

The daily tray opens after level 10. A new one starts at midnight, in your iPhone's own time zone and clock. There are no streaks, so missing a day costs you nothing. Daily trays have no flick limit.

What is Zen?

Zen opens after level 20: endless calm trays with no par and no counter. Use the switch at the top to choose Calm or Tricky trays. Every ten trays there is a short pause, so you can rest or turn the page.

Can I turn off motion, sound or vibration?

Yes, in Settings (the round button on the Home screen): Sound, Haptics, Appearance (System, Light or Dark) and Reduce Motion (System, On or Off). With Reduce Motion, shapes settle into their slots without sliding and water effects are calmer.

Does Pebbloom collect data or show ads?

No. Pebbloom has no ads, no tracking and no account, and it works offline. See the privacy policy.
